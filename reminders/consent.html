How Consent Is Collected

O'Sullivan Family Reminders · Last updated 1 August 2026

There is no web sign-up

This is a private family service. There is no public sign-up form, no keyword opt-in, and no way for a member of the public to be added. Recipients are members of my own immediate family. I add each number manually, by hand, only after that person has asked me directly to be added.

What each person is told before being added

Consent is given verbally, in person or by phone. Before any number is added, the recipient is told the following:

I'd like to send you reminder texts from O'Sullivan Family Reminders. They're personal reminders about tasks and appointments, the ones you've asked me to send. They'll come from the same number every time. You'll get no more than a few a day, at the times you pick. Message and data rates may apply depending on your phone plan. You can reply STOP at any time to stop them, or HELP if you need help. Your number stays with me and my messaging provider — it's never shared with anyone else, and you'll never get marketing from it. Is that okay with you?

A number is only added after the person agrees. Nobody is added by default, and no numbers are purchased, imported, scraped, or obtained from any other source.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them immediately. Reply HELP for help. You can also email [YOUR EMAIL] and I will remove your number and delete your reminders.

Related

The privacy policy confirms that mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The terms describe the service in full.
