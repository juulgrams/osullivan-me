What this service is

O'Sullivan Family Reminders is a private, non-commercial service run by PJ O'Sullivan. It sends scheduled reminder text messages — things like household tasks and appointments — to members of my own family who have asked to receive them. It is not a business, nothing is sold, and no messages are sent to anyone outside the family.

Who receives messages

Only people who have given their consent directly to me, in person or in writing, and whose numbers I have added myself. There is no public sign-up. No numbers are bought, collected, or added from any other source.

Message frequency and charges

Recipients receive a small number of scheduled messages, typically no more than a few per day, at times they have agreed to. Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile plan.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them immediately. Reply HELP for help, or email juulgrams@gmail.com.

No guarantee of delivery

Messages are delivered over mobile networks and can be delayed, filtered, or lost for reasons outside my control. This service is provided as-is, with no guarantee that any particular message will arrive or arrive on time. It is a convenience only and must not be relied on for anything time-critical, medical, or safety-related.
