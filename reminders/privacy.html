Privacy Policy

O'Sullivan Family Reminders · Last updated 1 August 2026

O'Sullivan Family Reminders is a private, non-commercial service run by PJ O'SULLIVAN that sends scheduled reminder text messages to members of my own family who have asked to receive them. This policy explains what information the service holds and how it is used.

What we collect

For each person who receives reminders, the service stores their first name, mobile phone number, time zone, and the text and schedule of the reminders they have asked to receive. This information is provided directly by the recipient or by a family member setting reminders up on their behalf with their consent. No information is collected from anyone else, and there is no sign-up form, tracking, or analytics on this site.

How we use it

The information is used for one purpose only: to send the scheduled reminder text messages that the recipient has asked for. It is not used for marketing, advertising, or any other purpose.

Who we share it with

Mobile phone numbers and message content are passed to Twilio, our SMS delivery provider, solely so that messages can be delivered. Twilio processes this information under its own privacy policy.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties for their own use.

How long we keep it

Information is kept only while a person is receiving reminders. When someone opts out or asks to be removed, their number and reminders are deleted.

Message frequency and charges

Recipients receive a small number of scheduled messages, typically no more than a few per day. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping messages
